about

tl;dr: I make sure vehicles behave the way they're supposed to, and fail safely when they don't.

I'm an automotive engineer working across systems engineering, simulation and functional safety (ISO 26262). I started out validating and durability-testing vehicle components at TVS Motor in India, moved through supplier engineering at Volvo Trucks, and came to Germany for a master's in International Automotive Engineering at TH Ingolstadt.

Since then I've run FMEA and FMEDA on safety-critical systems at Modelwise in Munich, and built thermo-mechanical FEM simulations, reduced order models and DOE workflows at THI's Institute for Innovative Mobility. I like turning simulation results into engineering decisions, and automating the boring parts with Python.
